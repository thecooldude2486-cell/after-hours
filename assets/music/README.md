Original instrumental compositions for After Hours v8. Local 24kHz mono PCM: school-piano is a warm original major-key school tune; empty-school-song is its unstable detuned version played diegetically in empty rooms; east-wing-drone is a pursuit layer. No speech or existing commercial melodies are sampled. All tracks use the adjustable music bus, which ducks beneath dialogue.


## v10 distress arrangements

empty-school-song-v10, maintenance-pressure-v10, pursuit-v10 and gym-memory-v10 are original authored 24-second stereo 24kHz PCM arrangements. They combine minor piano fragments, beating low tones, inharmonic partials and reflections with existing licensed CC0 metal/wood/cloth/creak/soft foley. No external song, recorded orchestra or human vocal performance was used. Source composer: workspace `work/sfx/score-v10.py`. Peaks are normalized to -1.2dBFS before the game's adjustable buses/master compressor. Friendly school-piano remains unchanged.
